## Survival calculations and future maps

Health recovery is shared in `src/simulation.js` through `HEALTH_RECOVERY_RULES`, `tickVitals()` and `healthRecoveryStatus()`. Without a food effect, fed players recover 0.5 health/second. Each confirmed nutritious meal adds an independent2 health/second layer, capped at3: total2/4/6, replacing the baseline while any layer is active. Effective nutrition ×0.8 /2 sets that food's lifetime, clamped to2.5–10 active seconds. At the cap, replace only the earliest-ending layer when the new food extends it; never shorten a stronger layer or extend the other two. Full health caps actual healing but retains finite timers. Empty hunger prevents healing and still causes7 damage/second; timers keep expiring. Pause/terminal states freeze updates, and a new round clears all layers. Integrate each expiry and the fed-to-starving boundary over actual active elapsed time, including long steps. Mouth, confirmed torpedo, companion, aerial meals and lord nutrition share this path. Supplies retain instant recovery and preserve meal timers; the once-only rare blessing retains its cap/fill and clears prior food layers. All caps use `vitalLimit(player)`.

`recoveryMeals` holds at most three `{duration, remaining}` entries. `mealRecovery` is only the existing healing-versus-growth nutrition allocation ledger: reserve up to effective nutrition ×0.8 against the unreserved missing health when a meal actually adds/refreshes an effect; actual healing consumes it, full health clamps it, and final expiry clears it. It cannot set the rate, combine lifetimes or prevent a full-health meal from adding a finite effect. Keep the existing growth/nutrition curves; food that cannot improve a capped effect must not charge another healing share. Synchronize both languages, help, Guide, notices and HUD. Show the current available recovery rate and layer count rather than a countdown, with three bounded independently depleting indicators; verify narrow and150-cap layouts and real timed meals.

Body size sets the shallow hunger cost: larger characters consume food faster. Deep-water acclimation additionally penalizes juveniles, so growing toward 18 m can reduce their absolute deep-water drain despite increasing their shallow cost. Keep this behavior shared by all maps in `src/simulation.js` through `hungerDrainRate()` and `HUNGER_RULES`, rather than adding independent per-map formulas. Current calculation, in hunger points per second:

```text
base = 0.22 + 0.02 * clamp(length - 3, 0, 3) + 0.03 * max(length - 6, 0)
depthPressure = clamp((worldDepth - 45) / (500 - 45), 0, 1)
juvenile = max(0, 1 - (max(3, length) - 3) / (18 - 3))
depthFactor = 1 + depthPressure * (0.5 + 4.5 * juvenile)
hungerDrain = 2 * base * depthFactor * optionalProfileMultiplier
```
