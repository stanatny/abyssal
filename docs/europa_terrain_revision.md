## Reference interpretation

- [NASA Europa Clipper: ingredients for life](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/europa-clipper/why-europa-ingredients-for-life/) describes possible chemical energy from water-rock interactions and potentially hydrothermal vents. It does not confirm vents or animals on Europa. The regional scene is compressed fiction.
- [NASA: chemical balance](https://europa.nasa.gov/news/10/europas-ocean-may-have-an-earthlike-chemical-balance/) also discusses serpentinization without requiring volcanic activity. Mineral crusts and chemical plumes are more appropriate than a confirmed red-lava landscape.
- [Europa Report production interview](https://www.fxguide.com/quicktakes/the-making-of-europa-report/) describes satellite/ISS-inspired spacecraft detail and octopus/squid probing anatomy. Author the damaged vessel and creature geometry locally; research captures are not bundled assets. A linked historical breakdown-video host failed DNS lookup, so it must not be reported as inspected footage.
