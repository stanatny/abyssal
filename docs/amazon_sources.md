# Amazon River art and ecology sources

This is an original fantasy river expedition. Two winding channels, a rainforest surface cap with a fantasy eroded underside, oversized lord pools and saltwater playable characters follow the user's sketch. It is not a geographical reconstruction or a claim that the real Amazon reaches these displayed depths. No third-party images, textures, recordings or models are bundled.

Discus, hatchetfish, armored catfish, arowana, stingray and freshwater-turtle models use distinct body plans, not recolored marine meshes. Forest geometry, buttress roots, vines, broad leaves, sediment, riverbed erosion and music are procedural originals. Shared fish-feeding Foley retains its existing documented CC0 provenance in [audio_sources.md](audio_sources.md).

## Redraw references

- [Tennessee Aquarium: Silver Arowana](https://tnaqua.org/animal/silver-arowana/): long compressed silhouette, large reflective scales, paired mouth barbels and posterior fins.
- [National Aquarium: Amazon River Forest](https://aqua.org/explore/exhibits/amazon-river-forest): flooded-forest habitat, submerged vegetation, turtles, catfish and rays. The vast island vault and supporting sediment columns are explicit fantasy geology, not a claim about normal Amazon banks.
- [Kolmann et al., 2016: freshwater-stingray feeding](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5031661/): continuous pectoral disc and ventral feeding anatomy, rather than a rigid plate covered with floating decorative spheres.
- [Smithsonian Corydoras specimen](https://www.si.edu/es/object/corydoras-aeneus%3Anmnhvz_5058780): museum taxonomic/specimen reference for armored catfish. The game's generic Corydoras is an original interpretation, not a claim to reproduce a particular specimen.

Anaconda proportions now follow a long slender body, low broad head and dorsal eyes/nostrils; the inherited 8.2 m observation size is unchanged. Titanoboa color and soft tissue, Stupendemys scute colors, and all colossal/lord details remain artistic reconstructions. All meshes, material patterns and motions are original procedural work; no reference image is included in the distributed assets.
