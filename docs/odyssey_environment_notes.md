# Odyssean Sea environment

## Scope and interfaces

The new environment lives entirely in `odyssey_ocean.js`, `odyssey_environment_geometry.js` and `odyssey_environment_landmarks.js`. It does not alter the seven accepted regions. `createOdysseyOcean(parent)`, `createOdysseyOceanAsync(parent, options)` and `createOdysseyOceanSteps(parent)` share the same actual construction generator. The returned object supplies `root`, `colliders`, `navigationColliders`, `heightAt`, `groundHeightAt`, `waterHeightAt`, `landmarks`, `radarPaths`, `wreck`, `wreckEntrance`, `update` and idempotent `dispose`.

`update(time, position, dt, highQuality, cameraPosition)` uses the owner's active clock and creates no animation loop or per-frame geometry. The authoritative `odysseySeabedHeight` and region extents come from `odyssey_config.js`. The water mesh and `odysseyWaterHeight` use the same two small swells. The refined sky, island, atmosphere and above-water state sample that actual height at the camera; the earlier `surfaceY - 1.5` sky threshold is superseded. Above water the shader is opaque and writes depth, while submerged views retain the accepted transmission. An eighty-unit strip batches seabed construction; subsequent yields cover each limestone section, instanced ecology batch, gateway, pottery cargo field, galley ribs/planking/fittings, water and offshore island.

The scene owns its geometry and materials. Immutable geometry is shared by its own instances; seagrass sways in the GPU with fixed roots. The existing eighty-unit instance clustering permits normal frustum rejection. Construction cancellation removes partially built nodes and releases tracked geometry. Static architecture is merged by material; terrain and procedural surface detail use the accepted shared helpers. No new texture, point-light pool, self-managed RAF, ecology reduction or global quality change is introduced.

## Design and terrain fit

The underwater route descends from pale Mediterranean sand through red branching coral, lace sea fans, golden sponges and seagrass toward darker limestone/basalt. The two broad Greek gateways have continuous fluted shafts, bronze collars and inlaid meanders, and an open triangular pediment frame. Each plinth extends from its lowest footprint sample to a level upper foundation; its column begins at that upper surface. The ninety-two/ninety-six-unit openings retain a wide bypass above and around the structure. They do not form whole-region walls.

The original ninety-metre galley has a keel, individual ribs/planks, end decks, damaged central hull, broken mast, bent bronze stems, surviving oars, wheel and cargo amphorae. Its keel is settled near the seabed and the ship banks with the local slope. The same transform is applied to every physical timber collider, rather than drawing a tilted wreck over a different invisible box. Central southern shell and ribs are really absent. Low rocks nest the keel instead of artificially supporting it on tall pillars. The central hold is open and has no whole-ship enclosing collider; three-, sixteen- and thirty-metre bodies fit longitudinally in the tested interior position. The primary adult route remains outside this optional wreck.

The warm sky and distant white-cliff island patches are visible above water. Native integration review found that drawing those patches and the sky underwater produced flat grey horizon slabs and sliced silhouettes. They are now hidden underwater, leaving the game's authoritative underwater background. The water shader uses the real scene fog and restrained Snell-window transmission. Island patches lie outside playable bounds, so they are scenic offshore silhouettes rather than unsupported walkable land. Detailed living reefs and ruins remain in the real underwater space.

All geometry, materials, motifs and placement are procedural interpretations. They are not downloaded movie or commercial-game assets. Shared quality references and the map's mythology sources are recorded in `odyssey_brief.md`; these development references are not player-facing attribution copy.

## Focused verification and evidence

`tests/odyssey_environment.test.js` covers actual rendered floor rays, staged preparation and abort cleanup, bidirectional grown-body primary-route collision, optional wreck interior clearance, same-sampler water, underwater sky state, frozen clock updates and independent map resource ownership. Four focused tests pass. Ninety floor-ray samples, including edge shelves, differ from the authoritative formula by at most **0.134 world units**. Controlled thirty-metre bodies traverse the declared primary route in both directions without sampled blockage. These mathematical checks do not replace native character-control traversal of every optional route.

Ignored evidence is under `.local/odyssey_review/environment_*`. The standalone wrapper captures surface, nursery, garden, wreck, close wreck and deep basin, plus three 390×667 views. It uses controlled lighting/fog and no live ecology; it is not the real gameplay lighting or a populated benchmark. Its final pre-backdrop-fix scene inventory ranged from about 67k to 435k submitted triangles and 35–164 draws in the recorded desktop views. The full static scene contains **716,200 triangles**, **1,298 decorative instances** and **430 individual colliders**. Those totals include mutually distant pieces and are not simultaneously visible draw counts. A synchronous Node construction inventory observed a largest stage of about 10 ms; that is not browser frame, startup or physical-device heat evidence.

Native home and nursery review verify the corrected underwater backdrop in the integrated scene with zero observed console/page errors. `environment_native_home.png`, `environment_native_nursery.png` and `environment_native_phone_nursery.png` record that pass; the final narrow image is a 390×667 viewport, not touch emulation or a physical phone. The owner's populated native traversal, pause/restart, touch behavior, resource plateau and serial performance review remain the authoritative full-map delivery evidence. Do not infer complete quality or device acceptance from the standalone wrapper or these focused units. No commit, push, merge, dist build or service replacement is performed by this environment contribution.
