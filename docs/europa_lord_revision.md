# Europa Arm Combat and Remaining Soft Appendages

Status: development candidate after local checkpoint `55f999f`; no push or formal publication is authorized. The preceding continuous-arm/Kraken/Azure work was accepted and committed locally. Published v0.10.1 remains unchanged.

## Encounter identity

Lumen Stalker no longer accelerates its entire body along the generic charge path. **Luminous Arm Lances** locks a nearby landing point after a 2.4-second warning, then extends three real, continuously skinned arms in sequence. Landing points remain fixed throughout the final 0.8-second warning interval and the attack. Each arm has 0.3 seconds to extend, a 0.12-second hold and a 0.38-second retraction, starting 0.5 seconds apart. Swept real arm tips deal at most one 38-point base hit per cast; retraction does not deal damage. Sideways or vertical sprint evades the locked paths, and solid-world occlusion blocks damage. Range is 88 world units; the 1.8-second attack is followed by four seconds of recovery.

Abyss Weaver retains its **Tidal Loom** identity, but the three pressure sectors occupy different heights rather than one flat ring. A 2.4-second warning shows their upper/lower edges; a 2.6-second sweep rotates the bands with its forked arms, then gives four seconds of recovery. Sector heights relative to the locked layer are 0, -14 and +14 world units, with 10-unit half-height, 18–85-unit radial extent and the existing angular gaps. The locked plane may approach the target's height by at most 20 units. Each cast deals at most one 32-point base hit. Sector gaps, vertical evasion and solid cover remain legal escape routes; there is no unavoidable whole-territory blast.

Ordinary close-contact bites remain during territorial hunting, but neither specialized attack stacks a generic proximity bite on its own hit. Both preserve body length, home territory, ecology/food, 25 m eligibility, three independent valid attacks and once-only regional victory accounting. No extra objective or ability damage is added to the other guardians.

## Appendage inventory and anatomy

The earlier checkpoint already corrected the actual octopus, cuttlefish, Crown Filterer, Kraken, Weaver and Stalker factories. Playable Giant Squid's eight arms/two tentacles and mantle-first propulsion already use continuous animation and are retained. This pass additionally binds Cameroceras's eight modeled arms, the rare Pearl Nautilus's twelve represented feelers, Spiral Grazer's two feelers and Bell Carrier's six colony stems to continuous instance-owned bone chains. Satellite bells remain attached to their bending stems. These are distinct structures, not twelve recolored octopuses; shells and bells remain rigid where appropriate.

The real [chambered nautilus](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/animals-the-ocean/animals-a-to-z/chambered-nautilus) has tentacles without octopus-like suckers. The [aquarium's cephalopod overview](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/animals-the-ocean/animals-a-to-z/cephalopods) describes over ninety nautilus tentacles; the twelve in our small fictional rare are a visible representation, not an anatomical count claim. Cameroceras soft appendages remain a speculative reconstruction; the [museum's fossil discussion](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/news/2026/april/worlds-oldest-octopus-fossil-isnt-octopus-after-all.html) explains how nautiloid soft parts are usually missing. Alien arms and pressure weaving are original imagined biology, not observations of Europa. No external model or texture is copied.
