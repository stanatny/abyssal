## Scope and decisions

- Audit actual population data and Guide membership together. Every region must have at least three ordinary kinds in each of the shoal, modern-hunter and ancient categories, with at least one region-exclusive kind in each. Shared animals remain intentional; exclusivity does not require replacing the entire food chain.
- Add a distinct, slow Archelon to Hawaii and a barrel-bodied Shonisaurus to Mariana, the two missing exclusive ancient categories. Their original procedural models reuse the accepted skin/fin/animation pipeline, with independent proportions. Preserve real-size and reconstruction qualifications.
- Inspect nutrition and threats at 3/6/10/16/20/25/30 m (Mariana starts at 15 m). Keep the shared hunger, healing, growth, nursery, respawn and combat rules. Bermuda needs an intermediate 11–12 m food bridge between its 6 m animals and 13 m mosasaurs; use existing polished pliosaurs and plesiosaurs offshore.
- Hawaii: 30 m and any one of its two selected lords. Atlantis: 30 m and consuming the sacred pearl in the existing lower Poseidon vault. One randomly selected, undisclosed guardian among its three independent Krakens unlocks this pearl; other defeats cannot substitute. Bermuda: all four local lords defeated. Mariana retains 30 m, its four ordered seals and arrival at the bottom refuge.
- All selected lords are persistent for the round. Defeated lords never relocate or respawn; ordinary prey still respawn. Returning home starts a fresh expedition and fresh hidden Atlantis selection.
- Show destination identity, difficulty, food progression and completion in selection and Guide. Keep the regional objective readable in the HUD without a new large overlay. Check English/Chinese, desktop, 390 px and 320 px, keyboard focus and touch-sized controls.

## Reference and integration contract

Archelon reference: [Natural History Museum](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/giant-prehistoric-turtles.html), including its leatherback-like shell and huge flippers. Shonisaurus reference: [Nevada State Museum](https://www.carsonnvmuseum.org/june-curators-corner/), approximately 50 feet (15 m); full-body details and revived extreme-depth habitats remain artistic reconstruction. No external mesh, image or sound is bundled.

Use the released turtle and accepted ancient ichthyosaur as quality comparisons, rather than enlarging them. Preserve `createCreature` normalization, -Z head direction, shared immutable geometry/materials and independent motion. The pearl is an original magical exploration object, not ordinary food or a general reward: it cannot add growth, disappear before eligibility or activate a different region's completion. Its contact and visual position must agree in the actual crypt and remain reachable by both characters at 30 m.

Implementation evidence and remaining limits follow below. Automated budget models and staged native fights do not establish natural whole-round pacing or physical-phone performance.

`getRegionSpecies()` remains the source of truth for spawning, membership and Guide filtering. The union contains 47 ordinary kinds; the Guide has 65 entries including playable characters, lords, humans and landmarks, with rewards kept separately. This revision does not pretend every kind is exclusive or add a new exclusive fantasy lord: Atlantis deliberately keeps its three independent Krakens, and Mariana its accepted four species of gatekeeper.

Using actual `consumePrey()` with full health and an empty hunger bar, a 25 m adult obtains 70 hunger from megalodon in every region. At 500 world-depth units, drain remains 1.105 points/s: that meal covers about 63 seconds without another meal or travel damage. A 30 m adult obtains 86 hunger from Ichthyotitan, covering about 66 seconds at 1.3 points/s. At 20–25 m every region retains a larger 28 m ordinary Ichthyotitan threat, in addition to local lords; at maximum size the lords remain the intended apex threat. Juvenile nurseries remain protected, with small school meals and outer-reef threats accessible progressively. At 3 m Atlantis's dense spadefish/sardine schools provide 8 hunger per successful school member, rather than assuming every small individual must have a high unscaled nutrition value.

These calculations use the unchanged shared size-efficiency, school-efficiency, healing and cubic-mass rules. They quantify individual feeding events and available configured layers, not encounter probabilities or natural whole-round duration. Food in an unopened Mariana layer does not count as an immediate meal; accepted upper-layer 11–20 m stock remains available before Hydra. The Bermuda bridge adds four 11 m pliosaurs and four 12 m plesiosaurs offshore, without putting hunters in the nursery. Successful lord bites still restore eight hunger each.

## Implementation and evidence

- `expedition_objectives.js` owns distinct per-round completion state; region selection, Guide, late HUD and results read its configuration. A hidden guardian is drawn anew on each real Atlantis expedition. Disabled/foreign instances and duplicate defeat records cannot complete Bermuda. The pearl cannot complete before unlocking or below 30 m, and its collection grants no nutrition or growth. All lords hide immediately on defeat, including the final winning frame; normal prey replenishment remains intact.
- The original pearl altar uses carved stone, bronze inlay, a ribbed nacre cradle and a fixed-position emissive pearl. It reuses the accepted city lighting, with no extra point-light pool or RAF. Its locked seal disappears on the genuine guardian event; radar guides to the vault only after unlocking. Reset and idempotent disposal are covered. The altar is decorative; it does not add a new movement barrier or alter the existing adult crypt routes.
- Original Archelon and Shonisaurus models render 8,936 and 8,216 triangles respectively, each in ten meshes. Continuous anatomical lofting, surface detail, independent jaws/flippers and shared immutable assets use the accepted ancient pipeline. Archelon's shell remains rigid while four flippers move; Shonisaurus's body/tail deform. Both normalize to their configured length and retain the existing feeding/effect/audio paths. Seven previous ancient models keep their original geometry.
- Actual game rosters were checked across all four maps and a return cycle: every configured kind instantiates, all sampled 293/392/321/313 spawn positions pass the real solid/depth checks, and no wrong-region kind leaks in. Both new exclusives were visible at normal follow-camera scale, captured through the actual feeding loop and restored at their own anchors after the ordinary 28-second timer. Eight live-swim frames per model and Guide views were inspected; this is staged render review, not a natural exploration session.
- Controlled native lord fights validated Hawaii's growth-plus-defeat ending; both Orca and Squid Atlantis locked/wrong-guardian/sub-30 rejection and real pearl contact; Bermuda's four required three-hit fights at 25 m; 400-second no-respawn advancement and new-round reset. The Mariana browser regression again validated all four native fights, closed/open adult passages, 30 m bottom-only completion, reset, resident feeding/respawn and regional isolation.
- Destination cards show identity, difficulty, food layers and objective. Long mobile overviews are collapsible; the objective is outside that disclosure, and departure/Guide actions remain reachable in a fixed bottom bar. Scrolling content avoids the header. The Guide's independent region selector has its own overview. English/Chinese UI was inspected at 1440×900, 390×667 and 320×568 with no horizontal overflow or console errors; one initially untranslated Guide count was corrected. Mobile views are browser emulation.
- Serial headed Chrome samples on this Mac's Apple M2 Pro, 1440×900 CSS pixels, High quality, fixed positions and 120 frames per region measured approximately 59.9 / 60.0 / 59.0 / 60.3 FPS, with frame p95 19.3 / 20.1 / 18.8 / 19.0 ms. These are short current-scene samples, not a matched optimization benchmark or physical-phone performance claim.
