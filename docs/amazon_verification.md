## Visual, ecological and ownership decisions

The forest uses shared merged trunk/branch/vine/leaf assets with instanced 100 m chunks. Submerged roots have solid capsule proxies; bank collision rows extend below the deepest pool so diving cannot bypass the divider. Warm tannin water, eroded ochre/mud banks, floating leaves, river grasses and sediment replace ocean coral and active fleets. Ordinary channels remain open; the two enlarged deep pools accommodate lord encounters.

Small fish use different body plans rather than a single recolored marine model. Flat disks, hatchet bellies, armored catfish/barbels, upturned arapaima mouths, ray disks, web-footed turtles, eel fin waves, articulated snakes and plated crocodilians have distinct animation. Static color geometry is shared and batched; small detail tessellation is bounded while the larger continuous bodies retain their near-view shape. Approximate model costs: Neon Tetra 3,992 triangles / five draws; Amazon Discus 4,072 / five; Black Caiman 15,848 / nine; Rootjaw 17,168 / nine. These are model counts, not whole-frame draw calls.

Construction yields between bounded terrain/forest batches through the existing loading coordinator and construction ownership scope. Map disposal removes owned meshes, instancing allocations, geometries and materials once; immutable creature caches remain shared. Ecology/feeding/respawn use declared regional traits, not a separate Amazon simulation. The optional reward-anchor and radar-path capabilities preserve the other maps' default behavior.

Read [sources and adaptations](amazon_sources.md) for provenance. Saltwater Crocodile is explicitly introduced fantasy fauna. Fossil sizes/reconstruction, inflated readable small-animal sizes, the revived food chain and compressed deep-water pools are qualified game adaptations. No external art, texture or music asset was bundled; accepted shared feeding Foley retains its prior documented provenance.
