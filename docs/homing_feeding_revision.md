## Implemented behavior

Torpedoes acquire the same unobstructed candidate displayed by the reticle, within 7 degrees and 140 units. At launch they point toward it, then continuously home toward that one target with bounded steering at 240 degrees/second. Lateral dodges outside the former nine-degree corridor no longer cancel assistance. Retirement/defeat and obstruction release the lock; there is no automatic target change. Player-distance mesh culling alone is not death. Hits still require actual swept contact; walls/terrain and finite travel remain authoritative. The 2-second cooldown, fixed 10-health/10-stamina sacrifice, one/three-hit ordinary rules, 25 m lord gate, three-hit lord settlement, pause clock and bounded two-projectile pool remain intact. Both-language reticle, selection and Guide copy describe target lock.

Five-region ecology now passes through `spreadFeedingSchools()`: medium roaming schools split into smaller groups offset across the region width, preserving population, assigned depth, nutrition, growth and exclusive kinds. Fixed nursery, city/gallery, wreck-interior and independently resident anchors are excluded. The three accepted wreck-interior groups use the shared `fixedHabitat` metadata, keeping their 14 food animals on the existing gallery routes instead of spreading them out of the hull. If a roaming species declares more habitat anchors than its old large school count, its existing inventory is divided across all declared anchors before lateral distribution. This recovers five Europa species whose third or deeper feeding habitats previously received no school. Registry data drives initialization, migration, habitat navigation and respawn consistently. Ordinary predators without explicit anchors also spread across three lateral lanes instead of one narrow central lane. Explicit regional anchors and limited outer-reef predator territories stay authoritative. Migration uses species visibility plus formation clearance instead of a fixed 220-unit threshold; placement remains outside visibility and within legal habitat.

Hawaii additionally retains the central route and fills both outer-shelf wings with medium Sunfish, Bluefin Tuna and Manta Ray groups, broader Archelon resident homes and two extra Dunkleosteus deeper on the transition. It gains 38 ordinary animals: 313 → 351. Atlantis stays 412, Bermuda 341, Mariana 333 and Europa 247; their roaming schools redistribute existing stock rather than inflating it. No new species/assets, starvation easing, food-value increase, cross-map substitution, nursery predators or lord respawn is introduced.
