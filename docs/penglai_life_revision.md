## Changes

- White Tiger now has an independent feline model: a long muscular torso, scapula and haunch transitions, broad short skull, paired whisker pads, rounded ears with rear ocelli, fitted irregular flank stripes, articulated fore/hind legs, four-toed paws and a long tail. Crouch, extended fore-paw leap and recovery use independent joints. Gameplay length remains 48 m. Ground animals and upright lords turn about world up instead of using an arbitrary shortest 3D rotation; a vertical target cannot flip the tiger. The ground charge direction is planar while its height follows the existing bounded leap arc.
- Aerial formations use moving targets **and their flight tangents**, rather than slowing to a tiny orbit's stationary slots. Cloud Crane / Bifang / Gudiao cruise configuration is 17 / 20 / 24 world units per second; these are fantasy game values. Larger wing-clearance slots, altitude lanes and a sparse near-neighbor separation grid address within-flock and between-flock crowding. Continuous native navigation, terrain/solid blocking, capture and habitat respawn still apply. Airborne orientation has stable yaw and bounded pitch. School replenishment uses the current flight formation, rather than its old central point. Ordinary predators patrol independently instead of combining a shared school target with conflicting personal homes; this keeps northern Hujiao in their actual assigned pool. The pooled aerial-neighbor references and ground states clear on population reset.
- The northern lotus pool gains three real water-layer carp schools and two more Hujiao homes. The shared density registry produces **27 added 6 m carps and nine added 16 m Hujiao**, bringing ordinary stock from 418 to **454**. No species, food reward, hunger formula or automatic recovery bonus changes. These schools are on the pool rim, with ordinary meals, healing-first allocation and normal habitat respawn.
- Six continuous spiral stone paths connect mountain slopes to six actual summit pavilions. Paths use the same terrain sampler; trees/outcrops leave route clearance. This intentional path clearance leaves 489 ground peach trees versus the preceding 522, with all thirty floating-island trees retained; it is not a food or performance reduction. Pavilion corner foundations reach the real slope. The main monastery receives plaster/wood walls, closed doors, fitted lattice details, a closed upper chamber and dark eave soffits. Curved roofs now have closed edges and undersides, with matching solid volumes. Its existing external colonnade, plaque and physical ward remain.
- The Sword Sage alone has an explicit persistent-pursuit capability. It activates after the four guardians are defeated and the player first enters his domain; he then continues his slower pursuit outside the home radius. Sprint can gain distance. Terrain still blocks movement and sword/dash contact; warned abilities and recovery windows remain. New state/reset and defeat clear this behavior. Other guardians remain territorial.
