## Model scope

The complete shared-factory atlas covers 132 models, including two alternate mythic forms. The two existing human kinds retain their separate factory. This review rebuilds weak shared silhouettes rather than claiming that every accepted creature was redrawn.

Nine shallow marine kinds have independent axial profiles: anchovy, herring, sardine, mackerel, flying fish, reef fish, bumphead parrotfish, humphead wrasse and tuna. Slender pointed heads, compressed deep bodies, rounded bellies, forehead humps, mouth shapes and tail peduncles now differ geometrically rather than relying on surface patterns. Median fins and pectoral roots follow the sampled body surface. Mackerel has separate dorsals and finlets; the parrotfish has a steep forehead and beak, while the wrasse retains thick lips and a rounded tail. Boxfish, turtle, sunfish and ray already have distinct accepted body plans and remain intact.

Penglai's jade minnow, Chiru, spirit carp and Dragon-Gate carp also receive different profiles: needle-like, deep flattened, elongated veil-tailed and deep gold-carp bodies. Their fitted pectoral membranes point rearward and their fin rays follow the same geometry. Wenyao and Luo retain their already distinct wingfish forms.

Kun retains its 40 m gameplay length but gains a broad giant head, continuous pale jaw and belly, cloud-marked ridge, broad flippers and horizontal crescent flukes. Peng has a hooked beak, heavy chest, talons, continuous shoulder mantle, layered wings and fan tail. The ordinary Dragon-Gate Cloud Dragon is red-gold with four legs, modest horns and a carp-derived tail; it remains visually and mechanically weaker than the Azure Dragon guardian. All assets use the same world/Guide factory, cached geometry and individual continuous animation. Multiple posed close views inspect attachment continuity, not just vertex finiteness.

## Actual transformations

Penglai keeps eighteen initial ordinary kinds and the same 441 ordinary individuals, plus its one regional rare. Two existing Dragon-Gate carps and four existing Kun can change form; Guide entries for Peng and Cloud Dragon do not seed extra individuals or enter other regions.

The two selected carps live just before the actual Dragon Gate opening. After at least 18 active seconds, a nearby observer within 170 world units can see a 3.2-second rising leap, followed by a 1.4-second reveal. Its cloud-dragon form lasts at least 42 seconds. The dragon is 24 m, an ordinary predator that can injure characters below 25 m; reaching 25 m suppresses its retaliation. It does not count toward the monastery ward or guardian objective. Its territory is local to the gate, avoiding inherited ocean-roaming bounds.

Four Kun can rise and change into Peng after at least 26 active seconds nearby, with a 2.5-second ascent and 1.8-second reveal. Peng remains 40 m and holds its form for at least 32 seconds. Both transformations use staggered waits and refrain from starting while chasing. Return waits for disengagement and at least 60 units of separation, preventing a hunter from shrinking during a close fight. Solid-world checks can defer an ascent. Pause freezes phases and motion.

Each transformation retains the same entity and root mesh. Existing torpedo locks, contact, sonar and once-only feeding settlement continue to refer to that entity. Transition frames do not initiate attacks. Hidden normal prey restore the original species before habitat-aware respawn; new expeditions restore the base form before population accounting. Alternate meshes are prepared once for six eligible residents, never constructed per frame. Only eligible entities enter the controller; shared movement callbacks are reused.

## Guide and input

Opening the Guide focuses its close button rather than the search field. Mobile users can inspect it without an automatic keyboard; tapping search still focuses the input, and closing restores focus to the original trigger.

Playable-character records retain separate active and passive objects from the character registry. Two distinct panels appear before the long biography. Each shows its own name and effect; the active panel additionally shows cooldown and keyboard/touch input. Search accepts either skill name in both languages. No skill costs, cooldowns or effects change in this presentation follow-up. Two stale fixed-100 recovery descriptions now refer to the current vital limit, consistent with the accepted rare blessing.

Player-facing stories use **Background story** and **Myth and legend**, with direct individual narrative rather than authorship declarations or film references. The Sword Sage's user-directed history and Skybearer Sword Sect remain. Development provenance stays in source notes; natural-history and uncertain fossil information remain distinguished from imaginary life. Both languages, headers, rare metadata and affected regional Guide copy are synchronized.
