## Evidence and interpretation

NASA describes a likely saltwater ocean beneath Europa's ice shell, not a discovered ecosystem. Its January 2026 Juno report estimates roughly 29 km of conductive ice in the observed region, with composition and deeper-layer caveats. This is not a global exact thickness, and the game's dimensions are deliberately compressed. Large aliens, their emission and behaviors are fictional. Ice-ocean geology and potential chemical-energy habitats inform the setting; sunlight reaching the ocean floor and confirmed extraterrestrial animals must not be claimed.

Primary reference pages, read for this plan:

- [NASA: Juno's ice-shell measurement](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/juno/nasas-juno-measures-thickness-of-europas-ice-shell/).
- [NASA Europa Clipper: evidence for an ocean](https://europa.nasa.gov/europa/ocean/).
- [JPL: potential habitability and interior illustration](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/can-life-exist-on-an-icy-moon-nasas-europa-clipper-aims-to-find-out/).
- [Unknown Worlds: Below Zero's Snowfox update](https://unknownworlds.com/en/news/subnautica-below-zero-snowfox-update): distinct vent biome and predator identity; borrow the method of tying ecology to terrain, not a creature or asset. The direct creature image download was denied; no claim of inspecting that full-resolution image.

## Regional experience and geometry

The camera starts **already underneath a massive ice ceiling**. No sky dome, sunny waterplane, coastline, palm trees or surface breach is present. The ceiling's underside has overlapping pressure folds, reddish mineral seams, frost facets and deep pendent ice roots. A few inhabited seams provide soft local glow; they are not daylight windows through kilometers of ice. Ambient fill preserves playability and is an artistic adaptation.

World bounds: X `[-300,300]`, Z `[-1000,140]`, maximum world depth `900`; displayed depth uses the existing factor of four, measured beneath the local ice-water datum. The visual region is a compressed habitat slice, not a full moon or scientifically scaled ocean. Lowest ground transitions from a sheltered shelf near −50 to approximately −840; the upper solid ice boundary is near world Y=4. Use a real collidable roof with a conservative full-body upper bound. An under-ice surface adapter must prevent breaching and atmospheric effects. Actual steep upward roof contact should briefly ease the heading back to level, mirroring the accepted comfort behavior without changing ordinary Earth's underwater ceiling handling.

Mandatory passages need at least 70 m width and 50 m height, with 85 m turning diameters. Verify actual 30 m Orca and Squid passage both ways, including corners, ceiling, floor and follow camera. Keep the nursery in the existing safe coordinate band; predators and lord territory cannot overlap it. Match visible ice/rock surfaces to solids, including roofs and overhangs. The ground sampler owns only the lowest floor; it must not project players onto the top of an arch.

The palette is ivory ice, dark petrol-blue water, rust mineral folds, restrained amber feeding colonies and localized cyan/violet organs. Primary silhouettes should be visible at ordinary follow-camera scale. Distant landmarks remain readable, but small bioluminescent points fade with distance. Avoid white overexposure, scattered floating glow balls, an all-neon scene and repeating shiny flat planes. Close rocks/ice need shape variation plus material detail. Decorative attachment is checked across the root footprint, not only its center.

## Original lord: The Abyss Weaver / 星渊织母

Kind `abyss_weaver`; one persistent instance near `[0,-730,-745]`, territory radius approximately 140 m. Full axial length target 58 m, extended lateral span approximately 80–95 m. It is an original cephalopod-like alien, **not the Kraken with another material**: a split three-lobed mantle around a recessed pulsing organ, six muscular bifurcating arms, fine sensory fringes and an underside radial feeding aperture. No terrestrial beak/face mask, dragon head or armor crown. Body mass must remain readable when arms are extended. Idle motion, cruising, turning, warning, attack and recovery each have coherent articulation.

Use the shared territorial patrol/return, pause/reset and three separated flank-bite rules. A central continuous mantle provides legitimate side contact; feelers and broad empty gaps are not giant invisible damage proxies. Do not append the new lord to Hawaii's or Bermuda's default `BOSS_SPECIES.map(...)` population by accident: explicitly preserve their four original choices. Europa includes only its two alien lord kinds; the Lumen Stalker addition must not enter any Earth population.

They then sweep three separated sectors for roughly 1.8 s, leaving clearly visible escape gaps and vertical clearance. A hit does about 32 damage once per attack plus modest displacement, respecting cover and existing invulnerability; never lock or continuously drain a player caught in one volume. A 3 s recovery exposes the flanks. Reuse the existing phase machine and bounded effect pool, but drive both damage and visuals from the same sector geometry/time.
