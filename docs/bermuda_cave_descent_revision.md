# Bermuda slumber cave: descent and guarded-heart revision

Status: implemented and locally verified candidate, 2026-11-21. No commit, push, release or
version bump is authorized. This review supersedes the two-exit route, broad arm
opening and separate lava-jet presentation in the previous cave reviews. Preserve
their evidence as historical records.

## Production brief

The entrance should feel like the threshold of a colossal creature's ancient den.
Use layered weathered rock, overhangs and sparse existing bioluminescence at normal
follow-camera scale. Retain the accepted original Hugging Face sleeping titan,
submerged atmosphere, food and music. Do not add decorative exits or imply an
outdoor shortcut.

Only the northern entrance connects this lair to the sea. The internal route
descends around rock screens toward the deepest chamber, with sheltered dead-end
branches for food and observation. The other two Bermuda caves retain their own
through routes. Keep the existing map boundary and four combat lords. Match visible
rock, final seabed samples, roof and movement contacts, including adult turning and
full-body escape.

The three ordered seals remain. The heart rests within a real two-arm embrace,
accessible only during a short relaxed interval. Closing arms cause bounded,
readable crushing damage on actual contact; waiting outside their sweep is safe.
Collection continues to require the character's actual intake and clear access.
Taking the heart preserves the sixty active-second escape, stronger waking hazards,
fatal expiry and physical entrance closure. Food must remain obtainable along the
route without changing the shared survival formula.

Use the accepted Hugging Face lava maps on a thick, connected molten outpouring.
Wide irregular sheets should spill from the actual mouth toward locked, warned
areas; neither several separated tubes nor a purely visual widened fan meets the
brief. Damage, solid cover, visible width and the on/off phase share the same
geometry contract. Keep avoidable safe areas, falling rocks and the local pursuer.

## Implementation and checks

The main lair has one exterior north entrance. Two thick, irregular rock ribs
frame it, followed by a roof screen and a descending gate. The route bends twice
and descends 100 world metres to the heart chamber. The south and rear ends are
real permanent rock; the food branches end within the cave. After collection,
return through the north opening. The other two Bermuda caves retain their own
through routes and the map boundary is unchanged.

The accepted HF source meshes and PBR textures are unchanged. Independent
shoulder-supported arms now surround the heart. A 1.6-second relaxed interval
permits collection through the real character mouth; a 0.4-second closing warning
precedes real arm-contact crushing damage (30 health with a one-second interval).
Waiting outside the sweep is safe. No larger pickup radius or scripted collection
replaces the existing eight-metre mouth check. The one compact objective action
shows the current closed, open or closing state in both languages.

The attack uses a single closed, thick molten sheet, with a broad irregular
floor apron. Its 330 vertices, 140 cells and 840 tetrahedra are the shared source
for rendered skin and actual contact damage. Width is 48 metres while sleeping
and 64 while aware, with bounded narrower alternatives when real solid cover
requires them. A blocked sheet is omitted in its entirety, never shown as a cut
half-attack. Warning uses the same footprint. Actual lava damage remains 40;
falling rocks, the local pursuing predator, sixty active-second escape, fatal
expiry and physical entrance closure remain in effect.

The real world now appends the large cave contact collection in order, avoiding
Chrome's function-argument stack limit. Entrance contacts use bounded horizontal
slabs fitted to actual rock. The original southern hall roof datum is preserved;
only the actual northern uplift rectangle is excluded from the structural base
measurement. The rejected expensive SDF/voxel experiments remain local diagnostic
history and are absent from runtime code.

Population remains 816. Existing quantities, sizes, nutrition, depth rules and
protected lord supplies remain unchanged. Seven explicit resident/meal homes were
moved to clear new actual rock; original-roster tests restore only these recorded
coordinates before comparing the unchanged baseline hash. All 24 new tuna retain
their accepted homes. HF bones are seated on the actual sloped floor, and the
wall tablet follows the real inner rock surface instead of floating in the entry.

## Playable and delivery review

The current English live-scene recording shows the layered entrance from a wide
observer view, the descending turn, real closed/open/closing arms and one complete
warning/active/off sheet cycle. There are no page or console errors. The near
entrance take remains archived: its camera cropped a 135-metre opening at only
53 metres distance, hiding much of the irregular upper lip and rock roots.
Observer images use prepared adult positions, unlocked seals and labelled damage
protection to inspect the actual live phase cycle. They are not an unassisted
expedition. A first ordinary-damage pickup probe died after slow turning inside
the arm sweep and later pursuit; it is retained for route/control review rather
than hidden by changing health, invulnerability or the sixty-second deadline.

The second native-control run succeeded after the recorder released sprint and
started the turn immediately when collection occurred, before taking its blocking
screenshot. No game source was changed to obtain that result. Starting with a
prepared 25-metre adult and unlocked seals, the run used real mouth collection,
then ordinary damage/hunger, native keys and no healing, teleport or protection.
It exited the north opening, sealed it and secured the heart with 13.930 seconds
remaining, health 7.345 and hunger zero. The last near-death HUD remains visible in
the retained footage. This proves one controlled route, not a comfortable or
unassisted expedition, nor native escape completion by every character.

Serial headless Chrome measurements on this Mac at 1280×800 observed 71/72 frames
in seven-second High/Low samples: median frame interval 100 ms in both modes,
p95 166.7/116.7 ms, 57/43 draws and 838,004/800,236 rendered triangles. The sample
held a 30-second active-breath fixture for measurement; encounter phase timing
is unchanged. These are host observations, not physical-phone performance or a
matched comparison with the preceding headed sample. The dense cave remains
expensive. Physics caps its per-frame movement step while escape and vitals use
active wall time, so low frame rates materially increase difficulty; this work
does not claim that broader performance issue is solved.
