## Anatomy and movement

Karkinos now has a recessed oral field joined to its ventral thorax, overlapping mouthparts and eight articulated legs. A per-instance two-link solver fits actual sole anchors to the real sampled reef after its root transform and ordinary gait. Alternating swing and stance preserve a planted foot where reachable; the carapace tilts moderately with the slope. The caller applies a nonnegative body lift only when necessary, then refits once. This is bounded visual articulation, not a new universal physics system. Source geometry and shared materials remain immutable.

Crawler pursuit is horizontal and samples the next/look-ahead floor height before solid checks. It no longer aims vertically at a swimming target while forcing its center back onto the seabed. The original twin frontal claw sectors gain a marked 22 m inner frontal sweep; the rear and upper water remain safe. The 2.4-second warning, delayed once-only 30-point base hit, reef cover and 4.5-second recovery remain. Thus small players resting against the front carapace no longer sit in an unmarked blind gap. The 25 m player damage gate and three distinct hits required to defeat each guardian are unchanged.

Ceto Serpent, Styx Ringmaw, Tide-crowned Naga and Siren Finweaver use a propagating lateral body wave with stable anterior anatomy. Scales, gills and fin curtains move on the same continuous skin instead of remaining rigid beside it. Hippocampus keeps its curled anatomy with a flowing hindbody. Mermaid tails use a vertical waist-to-tail wave rather than copying serpent locomotion. Gran Maja now propagates a stronger lateral curvature wave through its existing six-joint body, with a fixed neck root and steady head. Effort eases through the existing accumulated animation phase; no independent clock is introduced. Its accepted anatomy, body size, skill and legacy contact root remain unchanged. Private bone textures now enter the existing idempotent retirement path. Earlier accepted Amazon/Chinese serpents remain intact.

The same review seats Ketos side trim on its actual loft surface and replaces coarse hovering Bronze Bastion Turtle scutes with subdivided plates sampled against the domed shell. Oracle Whale was inspected and retained; no demonstrated anatomy defect justified replacing it.
