## Changes and preserved rules

The model now combines navy and cobalt flank/shoulder armor, porcelain nose and fins, copper engineering bands, an angular low brow, paired range sensors and luminous gill circuits. Thruster cowls have colored shields and copper rings. All opaque decorations remain cached and material-batched. Physical length, launch/capture anchors, growth, movement and collision remain unchanged. Sprint plume geometry grows from 0.27 to 0.4 unit length and its full-power axial scale from 1.24 to 1.45: approximately 73% longer at full boost. The effect remains nonphysical, independently animated and absent at cruise. No new lights or animation loop are added.

The torpedo acquires one living, visible, unobstructed sea creature within 140 units and **7 degrees** of the firing direction. Its launcher initially corrects by at most **3 degrees**; subsequent steering is bounded to **22 degrees/second** and **9 degrees** from the original bearing. A retired, occluded or escaping target releases assistance permanently for that shot. There is no reacquisition, reversal or unrestricted homing. World/terrain collision still checks every flight segment. Flight-time visibility is rechecked roughly every 0.12 active-play seconds. An ineligible lord can be previewed as requiring 25 m but receives no aiming assistance or damage.

Preview and launch use the same selector and the actual animated launch point. A bracket identifies the candidate, with bilingual name, distance and estimated remaining hit count. This is an assistance candidate, not a guaranteed hit against a dodging creature. Ordinary target labels are suppressed while this preview is active. Text normally follows the bracket; if a narrow screen's radar obstructs it, text falls back below the central reticle. Brackets/text that overlap important controls or alerts are hidden. Preview selection is cached at 10 Hz on the existing active clock; reset, home and terminal transitions clear references and UI state.

Costs remain 10 health / 10 stamina, cooldown 5 seconds, range 140, speed 70 and blast radius 14. Smaller ordinary prey still take one hit; equal/larger ordinary creatures take three. Kills immediately use shared feeding rewards. Lords retain the 25 m threshold, three shared valid hits and permanent regional objectives. Defense, ecology and other characters are unchanged. English/Chinese Guide, help, selection descriptions and current contract are synchronized.
