## Changes and preserved behavior

The user reported excessive musical noise, weak pursuit contrast and overly similar soft-bodied fauna, then requested a luminous long-legged lord inspired by _Europa Report_. All sixteen ordinary models now separate crystal shells, jointed armor, radial petals, broad gliding wings, octopoid arms, coiled shells, bell colonies and armored serpents. They use separate propulsion and articulation rather than the same torso with recolored lobes. Guide descriptions and specimen colors match the new geometry in both languages. Sizes, all 247 ordinary instances, food values, habitat anchors, normal respawn, shared hunger/growth and collision rules remain intact.

**Lumen Stalker / 辉渊巡狩者** is an original 62 m long-armed alien lord in the Thermal Basin, with eight slender unbranched arms, a compact muscular mantle, concentrated light chambers and attached arm sensors. The [film-production interview](https://www.fxguide.com/quicktakes/the-making-of-europa-report/) describes octopus/squid influences, bioluminescence and probing behavior. This informed the silhouette and articulation; no film picture, model or texture is shipped. References are research, not runtime assets. No Avatar reference is used. The creature is fictional.

Its persistent territory is centered at `[-90,-465,-570]`, radius 105 m, outside the nursery. Slow territorial patrol continues at rest. The existing locked-charge phase machine supplies hunt, windup, attack and recovery: light chambers expand and arms gather during the tell; the locked attack can be dodged sideways or blocked by cover. Configuration uses 38 attack damage, speed 35, charge speed 88 and engagement range 135. Three separate validated flank contacts at 25 m or larger are required; it cannot be swallowed whole. The existing 58 m Abyss Weaver remains in the deepest Hollow, with its original six bifurcating arms and three-sector Tidal Loom. Europa completion now requires 30 m and **either** local lord defeat. Both are persistent and do not respawn in a round. Earth lord selections and all four Earth endings remain unchanged.

## Music and mixing

Europa exploration uses sparse 46-BPM glass harmonies and tonal low-pressure bass. Score scheduling no longer uses noise voices or noise-drum calls. Actual pursuit immediately introduces a separate 126-BPM eighth-note motif with semitone tension, short string accents and tonal bass hits; exploration ducks during combat and returns after escape. Lord engagement adds a lower layer and distinct onset cues. Missed scheduling intervals are skipped instead of replayed as an audio burst.

Only Europa scales the existing water ambience to 0.20 and current ambience to 0.08, and reduces music reverb wet gain from 0.26 to 0.11. Earth-region switches restore their original mix. Existing accepted feeding Foley is preserved. The Weaver's onset noise is shorter/quieter; Lumen Stalker's new attack cue uses glass harmonics and bass. These changes use the existing AudioContext, reusable buses, bounded voices and pause/mute lifecycle.

Actual OceanAudio offline rendering covers exploration (0–12 s), pursuit (12–20 s), lord/cues (20–25 s), pause (30–32 s), mute (34–36 s) and resume. Calm RMS is 0.00423, pursuit 0.03105 and lord 0.03830; pursuit-to-calm contrast is about 7.3×, versus 2.7× in the first pass. The lord-window peak is 0.2933, all samples are finite, pause settles to zero, mute peak is below 0.000016 and at most 18 voices are present. These measurements establish headroom and wiring, not listening approval.

A separate deterministic, music-muted ambience probe uses the same seed, 400 world-unit depth, actual `depth/740` update scaling and no pursuit. RMS falls from 0.01022 to 0.001882, approximately 82% lower. This measures quieter water/current ambience, not perceived sound quality. The actual game-path 40-second WAV and encoded MP3 remain in ignored `.local/europa_revision/` for user listening.

## Rendering costs and retained detail

Immutable geometry is shared; animated joints, jaw/wing/arm motion and luminous organs stay independent. Static opaque skin/armor pieces within the same joint use cached vertex-color batches, while emitting organs and animated groups remain separate. No ecology, visibility distance, collision, simulation rate or near-field art is removed to meet a cost target.

Ordinary models range from 1,732 to 13,744 triangles and 2–10 render parts. Weaver is 26,216 triangles/28 parts; the new Lumen Stalker is 44,556/46. The extra arm joints and light sensors are a cost tradeoff, not a performance optimization. No per-sensor point lights are introduced. Lumen's opt-in Guide radius fits its long arms on narrow screens without changing other previews.

Matched headed Chrome desktop samples use the current M2 Pro, 1440×900, DPR 1, 25 m Orca, seed 71523, full live populations at the Garden anchor `[110,-250,-365]`, 120 frames per quality. The comparison swaps only the ordinary-model source, preserving the same current map and two-lord setup. Original-model High samples before/after are 60.77/60.44 FPS, p95 19.2/19.3 ms, 148 draws and 437,394 triangles. The refined final High sample is 60.66 FPS, p95 19.2 ms, 178 draws and 487,414 triangles. Smooth is 60.48 FPS, p95 18.9 ms, 177 draws and 409,276 triangles. Short vsync-bound samples establish no FPS gain. The final more varied anatomy costs about 20% more draws and 11% more triangles in this view. Batching reduced the initial unbatched refinement from 272 to 178 draws while retaining the model detail.

A separate short 25 m Lumen-territory sample at `[-90,-465,-520]` measured High 60.74 FPS, p95 19.0 ms, 198 draws/358,630 triangles; Smooth 60.41 FPS, p95 18.7 ms, 99 draws/301,298 triangles. This is an encounter-area cost sample, not a controlled full fight or an original-model comparison. The later chamber-surface correction changes visibility/emission, not topology or draw parts.

These are short desktop samples, not physical-phone thermal evidence or an assertion that every lord encounter sustains 60 FPS. The first-pass Hollow spike and preparation costs remain recorded in the earlier verification. Scene-dependent measurements must not be substituted for each other.
