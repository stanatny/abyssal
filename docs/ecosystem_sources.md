# Ecosystem background and motion references

These are reference and provenance notes for the ecosystem candidate. Models and text are authored for ABYSSAL; no external models, illustrations or copied encyclopedia paragraphs are bundled. Short introductions distinguish natural history, fossil interpretation and explicit fiction. The links below support representative anatomical/behavioral statements, not every numerical game parameter or an assertion that every taxon received a full scientific review.

## Aquatic reptile motion

- [Smithsonian: Green Anaconda](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/green-anaconda): body markings, aquatic association and high-set sensory structures. The game reuses a long-bodied swimming silhouette without equating enlarged combat size with real adults.
- [Smithsonian: How snakes move without legs](https://www.nationalzoo.si.edu/news/news/how-do-snakes-move-without-legs): lateral undulation informs the body-wave approach. Joint frequencies and amplitudes are game animation choices.
- [Australian Museum: Estuarine Crocodile](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/reptiles/estuarine-crocodile/): muscular tail, short limbs, high-set eyes/nostrils and osteoderms inform fitted anatomy. Its distribution does not make it native to the Amazon.
- [Australian Museum magazine, volume 13 no. 6](https://media.australian.museum/media/dd/Uploads/Documents/30379/AMS368_V13-6_lowres.1b49d5b.pdf): tail-driven swimming, limb folding and slow hind-foot assistance inform procedural swimming. This anatomical account does not establish the exact motion of extinct/fantasy crocodilians.

## Living marine groups

- [NOAA: Killer Whale](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/killer-whale): toothed-whale identity, social behavior and acoustic navigation/foraging.
- [NOAA: Sperm Whale](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/sperm-whale): deep-diving, air-breathing marine predator and diet.
- [NOAA: Giant Manta Ray](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/giant-manta-ray): broad fins, cephalic lobes and plankton feeding. The Gilded Manta is an original rare, not a documented gold-colored subspecies.
- [MBARI: Barreleye Fish](https://www.mbari.org/animal/barreleye-fish/): transparent head and internal tubular eyes; forward facial openings are not a second eye pair.
- [MBARI: Deep-sea Anglerfish](https://www.mbari.org/animal/deep-sea-anglerfish/): modified fin-ray lure; the generic angler record does not imply that every angler species occupies the same depth.
- [Australian Museum: Octopus, squid and cuttlefish](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/molluscs/octopus-squid-and-cuttlefish-class-cephalopoda/): cephalopod arms, beak, eyes and ink.
- [Australian Museum: Sunfishes](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/fishes/molidae-sunfishes/): adult tail/fin morphology and different larval form.
- [Australian Museum: Pipefishes and seahorses](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/fishes/syngnathidae-pipefishes-and-seahorses/): family-level context.
- [Australian Museum: Great Barracuda](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/fishes/great-barracuda-sphyraena-barracuda/): elongated predatory morphology.
- [Smithsonian: Brown Pelican](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/brown-pelican): expandable feeding pouch and water draining. Generic pelican gameplay is not a complete species-specific field guide.

## Fossils, regional history and mythology

The existing [Amazon source ledger](amazon_sources.md) distinguishes living freshwater fauna, fossil-based Titanoboa/Purussaurus/Stupendemys and original river lords. The [Penglai ledger](penglai_sources.md) separates Zhuangzi, indexed Shan Hai Jing descriptions, traditional directional symbolism and game-specific adaptations. Earlier [lord anatomy notes](lord_anatomy_revision.md) describe Kraken, Hydra and Leviathan references.

Additional broad context used for restrained fossil introductions: [Smithsonian: whales through time](https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/marine-mammals/whales) and [Smithsonian: Meals of the Mighty Mosasaur](https://ocean.si.edu/through-time/ancient-seas/meals-mighty-mosasaur). Incomplete fossils do not justify treating exact color, soft tissue or movement as observed facts. New rare species, Europa organisms, technological/undead playable sharks, Gran Maja and Sword Sage are explicitly original game lore. Existing biographies use stable short family/taxon descriptions where no precise species-specific source has been checked; they avoid unsupported exact size or behavioral extremes.
