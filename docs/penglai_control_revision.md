## Diagnosis and change

- High-sky screenshot: previous marine bloom accepted negative depth above water (−0.37 at world altitude 312.5), an invalid visual parameter. However, the fresh pre-edit headed baseline did **not** reproduce the player's completely blank canvas at that altitude, eight positions/orientations or at world altitude 610. Do not claim this parameter is proven to be the complete cause. Clamp visual water depth at zero; follow the camera with the painted sky, disable its frustum/depth rejection and place its shader depth safely inside the far clip boundary. Ocean-depth appearance below the surface remains unchanged.
- Ground locomotion: the baseline's sampled ordinary roots retained world up, so a complete inverted root was not reproduced. Continuous retargeting during jumps, stale visual heading and swimming-time limb animation still caused implausible motion. Ordinary walkers now have rate-limited planar steering, a heading locked through warning/leap, smooth collision avoidance on subsequent steps and a gait driven by actual horizontal travel. The forelegs/hindlegs tuck/extend during a leap; Kui has an independent support joint. Feet, existing geometry, support clearance and collision remain authoritative. Respawn resets motion state. White Tiger retains its preceding upright guardian attack.
- Mechanical aim: the old neutral circle was 18 px and faint. It was at screen center, whereas the actual muzzle-forward ray projected 78 px above that center in one baseline fixture. Preview was also throttled to 100 ms inside a slower HUD timer. Camera view matrices updated only during rendering, after the HUD projected them: fresh testing found over 28 px of transient error after a turn (and a larger deliberate fixture-teleport error). Update view matrices before projection, put the sight on the muzzle-forward ray and refresh acquisition/feedback every active frame. Desktop uses a 42 px four-stroke crosshair, center dot and inner bracket; touch keeps its compact sight. White/blue/amber states retain names, estimated hits and eligibility text.

Acquisition still uses the same 7-degree forward cone and obstruction checks as firing. No acquisition charge, automatic target switching or widened cone is introduced. Tracking, range 140 m, cooldown 2 s, costs 10 health/stamina, ordinary two-hit durability and the 25 m / three-hit lord gate stay unchanged. Marine movement, food stock, rewards, hunger, progression and endings are unchanged. Character/Guide copy is synchronized in English and Chinese.
