## Delivered changes

- Seven regional fish/predator species and Crimson Sailfin use actual Hugging
  Face meshes. Cameroceras has the accepted researched orthocone orientation
  and soft-arm motion; uncertain extinct soft anatomy remains reconstruction.
  Previously accepted shared players, creatures and lords are retained.
- The Steel Cape freighter, tanker and offshore oil rig have new visual skins.
  Their existing movement, attacks and contact rules remain intact.
- The explorable liner has new funnels, windows, boilers, cabin furnishings and
  corroded steel/deck/bulkhead surfaces. Real hull breaches and collapsed walls
  have matching collision openings. Its four original routes, eleven rooms and
  204 interior fixtures remain; unsupported windows are removed.
- The ghost ship uses two actual HF prototypes for its hull and seven sails,
  preserving the captain, rigging, navigation and cannon rules. Seven existing
  waterspouts gain condensation, spray and foam. Their three attempted 3D cloud
  jobs failed: the shipped effect uses the successful background-removed cloud
  image with procedural animation, not a claimed generated cloud mesh.
- The lost aircraft and small cargo wreck have fitted local contacts and slope
  placement. Existing HF corals/fans/sponges cover 138 seabed placements; twelve
  mineral chimneys, two arches, 28 spires and sixty rocks use new HF prototypes.
  Instancing, shared leases and near/far detail bound the repeated geometry cost.
- The shallow arch and its marker move 65 m west off the former excavation cliff.
  Other geology landmarks and all non-geology contacts remain exact in the final
  pass. Fitted contacts and real adult traversal are tested; these are not exact
  triangle-mesh physics. No further balance or ecology changes accompany release.

Small vent fauna and broader seabed material polish remain optional future work.
