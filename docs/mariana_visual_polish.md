# Mariana surface, trench habitats and readable world boundaries

Status: uncommitted visual follow-up on `feature/mariana`, based on the existing 15 m / 305-animal candidate. Codex independently owns implementation and review. No commit, push or release is requested.

## Production contract

- **Surface identity:** a cool, overcast Pacific survey expedition, with an original research vessel (working aft deck, lifting gantry, instrument drums, antenna/radome, railings and lifeboat), a compact survey tender, instrument buoys, rugged volcanic island silhouettes and long low swells. Replace the Hawaii leisure fleet only in Mariana. Keep hull collision, shared 18 m ramming and normal surface/airborne controls. Distant islands are scenery beyond the navigable rectangle, not promised exploration destinations.
- **All-region boundaries:** show the existing four horizontal movement limits with localized current/suspended-sediment curtains, surface foam traces, and a clear radar turn-back indication. These are visibility aids for the existing bounds, not new damage, a smaller playable area or forced teleportation. Keep center views uncluttered, show the edge before contact and make the signal readable above water, deep underwater, in night/storm lighting and on small screens. Ordinary solid rocks continue to occlude the curtain.
- **Trench wall identity:** attached cream glass-sponge funnels, rust/coral anemone crowns, layered encrusting plates, brittle-star silhouettes and restrained blue living highlights. More detail around rock ledges and descent thresholds, with calmer sedimentary treatment at the deepest level. Preserve the existing openings, adult maneuvering envelopes, all colliders, fish habitats and food values. Soft small epifauna are non-blocking decoration.
- **Art references:** accepted Atlantis marine growth for attachment/material layering, Bermuda/Hawaii hulls for visible vessel detail, and existing Mariana continuous cliff meshes for support. Use the final rendered triangles for attachment; an analytic terrain sample alone is not sufficient. Biological motifs follow NOAA's [deepwater animal guide](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/data/animal-guide/) and [Mariana sponge observation](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/multimedia/daily-image-media-20210624/). Distribution, scale and luminous accents are authored fantasy scenery, not a claim that these communities occupy every real hadal depth. Original procedural geometry; no external image/model is bundled.
- **Rendering/lifecycle:** share geometry and materials, batch/instance repeated ornaments in existing vertical chunks, use GPU sway where appropriate, keep bounded lighting and existing update loops. Boundary geometry is reused across maps. A new fleet owns and disposes its resources; switching must not duplicate it or leave hulls behind.
- **Unchanged systems at the time of this visual pass:** 15 m start, 305 ordinary animals, four lords (then-optional surface Hydra), three deep seals, victory, nutrition, music, skills and existing non-Mariana ships. The later mandatory-Hydra gameplay revision supersedes only the old optional guardian / three-seal contract; see the current [map brief](mariana_brief.md).
