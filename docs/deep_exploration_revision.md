## Behavior

Bermuda now exposes the same wave-height equation used by its GPU water as a regional capability. Surface steering, floating, ballistic re-entry, launch impulses and final body-center limits sample that capability at the current main-loop phase. Floating follows both rising crests and falling troughs; a downward heading releases buoyant transport. Submerged movement, flat-water launch timing and Europa's ice retain their rules. Waves cannot provide a free charged launch.

Mariana keeps its bounds, total depth, initial size, population, nutrition, four ordered permanent guardian defeats and bottom-only ending. Its walls meander and fold instead of forming parallel rectangular faces. Four irregular, continuously meshed shelves surround alternating descent throats, with subdued matching organic pressure veils. Short eroded rock arches and attached ledges provide cover and return routes. Layer palettes distinguish upper ochre/green, blue galleries, violet folds and pale hadal rock. Cup sponges, anemones, brittle stars and encrusting colonies are larger and more legible, attached to the final triangle surfaces and instanced in the existing sixteen vertically culled sections. Their extreme-depth density and scale remain artistic fantasy. Hydra now guards `[-20,-525,-390]` in the first deep canyon, above the 650-world-unit seal; it is no longer a surface encounter in this map. Other maps retain their guardian locations.

Ordinary predators retain pursuit and contact damage until the player's absolute length advantage is at least 5 m. A valid player mouth capture settles first. A near-sized edible hunter can instead reach the player's flank or tail with its own directed swept mouth contact. A turned-away body, distant proximity or occluded pursuit cannot cause a bite. Nursery/territory permission, cooldown, feeding values and harmless ordinary prey remain unchanged. Near-sized hunters approach a side/rear position; existing rare skills still use their original telegraphs and direction limits. Sonar, target colors and Guide distinguish **Edible · Retaliates** from harmless food.

A small regional thermal-surge prototype provides size-independent deep-water danger. Three sites per horizontal region and two on Mariana's final mesh shelves use shared geometry and GPU plume motion. Their warm mineral-cloud plume warns for three seconds, erupts for four, then remains quiet for seventeen; active swept contact costs 12 health, with at least 2.5 seconds between hits per site. A cylinder 10 m in radius and 42 m high leaves a lateral bypass. Sites do not occupy nurseries, mandatory seal centers, the bottom refuge or Atlantis's treasure. Their resources follow regional reset/failure/disposal; no new RAF, light pool, audio context or food source is introduced. Damage uses the resolved movement and existing global invulnerability, and is disabled in post-win exploration.
