# v0.11.20 — Europa lifeforms, expanded ice ocean and research wreck

## Authorization and closing audit

On 2026-10-26 the user requests a final Europa review and asks whether the map
can be committed and pushed to main. The review finds no known release-blocking
unfinished item and proceeds under that conditional authorization. All 1,715
delivered input hashes and all 674 preview artifact hashes match before release
metadata edits. The remote main still matches the preceding v0.11.19 release,
`72ce40d`. No tag or separate GitHub Release is required.

## Integrated scope

- Actual Hugging Face exports cover all sixteen ordinary Europa lifeforms,
  three lords, Six-wing Crystal Seraph and the two rooted colony types. Fitted
  continuous motion, material retry/recovery and independent instance cleanup
  replace the old appearances. Existing written creature descriptions and the
  accepted four playable characters remain.
- The basin expands to 960 by 1,824 world metres with a configured 1,450 m depth
  bound. Layered terraces, ice/mineral surfaces and seep towers express a
  speculative chemical-energy habitat; they are not claimed as observed Europa
  biology or a literal-scale reconstruction.
- The 489.6 m research wreck has six equipped rooms, five staggered 53.1 m doors,
  a side breach and a smaller-body upper service loop. Adult turning/traversal,
  ground support and closed-wall visibility are verified. Duplicate wall faces
  causing flicker are removed; the constant three-light pool avoids a reproduced
  travel-time shader compilation hitch.
- The existing Lumen Stalker guards the bow with body-sized world navigation.
  Three-lord victory and 30 m growth remain. Each arena receives three dispersed
  feeding homes and an existing 29/32 m hunter able to threaten a 30 m player.
  Five hunter homes enclosed by the expanded hull move outside. Total stock stays
  352 ordinary animals plus the rare, with existing food values and replenishment.
- Opted-in swimmers gain continuous spacing, fitted body/terrain clearance,
  forward-facing turns and actual-travel-driven animation. Corrected packed
  normals remove the reproduced black blocks. Legal hunter pursuit is no longer
  overridden by patrol-home steering. This last shared correction retains
  nursery/territory rules and original attack values.

## Verification and limits

The final candidate passed 1,873 tests, formatting/build and bilingual public
flows. Existing native evidence includes a 30 m keyboard-steered 768.7 m traversal
through all six rooms, actual Lumen lash damage, real damage from each local
adult-threatening guard, real feeding/28-second replenishment and pause, repeated
region/resource ownership cycles, and matched wall-surface audits. Source/data
checks preserve all seven other maps' ecology and all Europa species' stock,
size, rewards and combat fields. These are dated candidate receipts, not fresh
release CI.

Fresh release tests/build, exact-main Node 22 CI, downloaded Pages artifact
comparison and native production flows are recorded separately under
`.local/release_v0_11_20/`. Verify the actual deployment SHA, `/abyssal/` resource
base, version label and same-address refresh before reporting publication.
Keep existing local services, logs and runtime archives; publish runtime files
only, without source maps or development hooks.

No physical-phone FPS, thermal, power or long-session memory claim is made.
Desktop touch emulation is not a phone test. The 59–60-meal stock calculation to
25 m excludes elapsed hunger, searching, injury and failed captures; controlled
combat/traversal does not establish natural whole-round completion or ideal
player difficulty. Those remain follow-up observations, not known broken
release behavior. The full-site artifact size is not one map's download size;
regional model chunks continue to load on demand.
