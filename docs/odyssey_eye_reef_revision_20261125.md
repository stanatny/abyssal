## Polyphemus: an actual recovery-eye counter

Polyphemus now declares `damageTarget: "eye"` and `counterPhases: ["recover"]`. His body remains physically present. Touching the chest, hands, feet, chin, brow or back of the head does not damage him, including during recovery. A flank position by itself no longer grants a hit against this guardian.

`polyphemus_weakpoint.js` derives the target from the accepted HF model's head bone and evaluates the current skinned outer-eye triangles. The fitted ellipse has radii 2.25 m and 2.16 m on the 90 m actor. This is a restricted surface patch, not a head-sized sphere, an enlarged body proxy or a replacement eye mesh. The helper rejects contact from inside or behind the skull, the closely spaced inner eye surface, nearer intervening skin, and world cover. The close-range marker is a visual outline of that same eye frame. It appears only during an unspent recovery opening, within 160 m and on the outward-facing side. It is removed on a spent opening, interruption, reset or defeat and never participates in damage or collision. Combat feedback and the bilingual Guide explain that the body blocked an attack and that the eye becomes available after the throw.
