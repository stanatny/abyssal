## Gameplay

Being grown does not remove ordinary threats: a30 m player can still be attacked by the28 m Ichthyotitan because the existing retaliation margin is5 m. Legal mouth captures still settle first; edible threats retain yellow feedback. This rule and all populations, meals, abilities, lord battles and regional objectives remain unchanged.

New terrain interaction uses proximity-triggered pressure mechanisms. Approach within52 horizontal world meters on the same local level to start a full3-second warning. The marked area then releases for2 seconds, followed by10 quiet cooling seconds. It cannot track the player, pull or stun them, close a doorway or grant food. A new activation always warns again. Each site emits at most one18-health event per activation; Mechanical Shark takes12 through its existing resistance, while global invulnerability may suppress a hit. Growth does not provide immunity.

Warnings identify the fixed lane or ring using fitted floor markings, small rising bubbles and a bilingual notice. The active footprint brightens; cooldown dims it. Players can cross after the release, dodge sideways, swim above its finite height or use actual solid cover. Both swept motion and terrain occlusion are checked. Reduced motion retains static particles/markings and active brightness rather than hiding danger. State advances only through active player movement, freezes at pause and clears on Home/new round/region disposal; post-win tours cannot take mechanism damage.
