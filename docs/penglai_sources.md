# Penglai Sanctuary — source ledger

Original regional assets were procedural work. The dated HF redraw below replaces selected meshes with original generated assets; texts guide features, and no museum image or commercial film asset is redistributed.

- Zhuangzi, _Free and Easy Wandering_: https://ctext.org/zhuangzi/enjoyment-in-untroubled-ease/zh — Kun transforms into Peng. Free-floating playable fish are an explicit game adaptation.
- Shan Hai Jing, _Western Mountains_: https://ctext.org/shan-hai-jing/xi-shan-jing/zh — Wenyao and Luo fish have winged forms; game sizes/flight behavior are adaptations.
- Shan Hai Jing, _Southern Mountains_: https://ctext.org/shan-hai-jing/nan-shan-jing/zh — Xuangui, Lushu, Hujiao and Gudiao inspire distinct anatomy. Mythological composite animals are not real biological reconstructions.
- Palace Museum, Four Symbols: https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/243822.html — east Azure Dragon, west White Tiger, south Vermilion Bird, north Black Tortoise.
- Palace Museum, Black Tortoise brick: https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/sculpture/233922.html — tortoise/serpent composite silhouette.
- Shiji, _Feng Shan Shu_: https://ctext.org/shiji/feng-shan-shu/zh — Penglai, Fangzhang and Yingzhou as sea mountains of immortals.

Jade minnows, lotus spirits, regional flight rules, giant combat sizes, the temple layout and sword-sage encounter are original fantasy inventions. The Dragon Gate is a folklore-inspired optional scene, not claimed as a Shan Hai Jing episode. Source uncertainty must be explicit in the Guide rather than inventing zoological facts.

## Redraw reference review

- Palace Museum, [Four Symbols](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/243822.html): the directional composition is used for actual habitats, not merely labels.
- Palace Museum, [Black Tortoise brick](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/sculpture/233922.html): the snake coils continuously around the domed tortoise shell. The great sizes and combat are game adaptations.
- Palace Museum, [Yun Shouping peach-flower painting](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/paint/231411.html): restrained pink/green variation and natural branching inform an original procedural grove; the painting itself is not redistributed.
- [Taiping Yulan, animal section](https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=en&node=407751&remap=gb) transmits the Lushu horse-form/tiger-pattern description. This review distinguishes a long equine head and neck from the feline silhouettes.

Search-index passages were available for the Chinese Text Project entries where full-page retrieval was blocked. Do not describe those pages as fully inspected. The original Sword Sage's forward face, brocade, crown and finger gesture are fantasy art direction; no classical text is claimed to specify this character. The Kui's hornless, single-legged bovine interpretation and the Bifang's single leg remain explicitly mythic anatomy, not zoology.

## Flight and guardian follow-up

The _Western Mountains_ distinguishes Wenyao's fish/bird-wing form from Luo's fish body, bird wings and voice. The new slender bronze Luo silhouette, two sharply swept wings and twin trailing ribbons are an original game interpretation, not a claim that the text specifies four wings or these materials. The [indexed original-text entry](https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=en&node=83569&show=parallel) was consulted where full-page access was blocked. The Four-Symbol compass habitats and tortoise/serpent pairing retain the museum references above. Speed, defense, damage and sword techniques are explicit game design, not attributed to an ancient combat specification.

`src/assets/penglai/sword_plaque.png` is an original 2048×640 transparent Canvas drawing of 以气御剑 over a wood/gold frame, using the locally installed Kaiti brush-style font at authoring time. Only the raster output is shipped; no font file, museum image or commercial artwork is redistributed. Its editable authoring script and runtime screenshots remain in ignored local review evidence. The floating islands, layered armor, flame mane and complete sword-riding anatomy are original procedural meshes through the shared model factory.

## Tiger anatomy and guardian flight repair — 2026-10-27

The [MIT Chongqing Zoo white-tiger photograph](https://dome.mit.edu/handle/1721.3/189688)
was downloaded and visually inspected for the short-furred neck, compact feline
head, rounded ears and relaxed tail. This static photograph supports anatomy and
rest posture; it is not evidence of a complete observed gait cycle. The image is
kept only in ignored research evidence and is neither shipped nor uploaded to
Hugging Face. The replacement concept is original generated artwork.

Bird/Azure Dragon bounded pitch and shallow banking are game steering choices
that address unwanted inversion and preserve readable pursuit. They are not a
claim that real birds can never perform rolls. Flight trajectory is validated
with actual world movement, not only isolated model animation.

## Wingfish, Lotus Sprite and Cloud Crane — 2026-10-27

The next batch uses actual historical illustrations as anatomical reference,
not a modern single "official" design sheet:

- [Anonymous Qing illustrated Shan Hai Jing, Scaly/Shelled Creatures V](https://www.shidianguji.com/book/7316439153875353650/chapter/1kibjypkmkf8y): the Wenyao and Luo text and scanned colored illustrations were read and visually inspected. Both combine a fish body with bird wings. Wenyao retains its white head, red mouth and darker markings; the narrower bronze Luo with two long tail lobes remains the game's established interpretation.
- [Wenyao woodcut from Sancai Tuhui](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sancai-tuhui-094-bird%26beast6-03wenyao-mottledflyingfish_-_labeled.jpg): the actual scanned illustration was downloaded and viewed to compare fish anatomy, wing roots and feathers. Its old composition is reference, not a runtime texture.
- [Palace Museum publication reproducing Zhao Ji's Auspicious Cranes](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2022/12/05/u638dad8fb05fe.pdf), PDF page 26 / printed page 113: the flight and perched silhouettes were inspected. The painting is attributed to Zhao Ji, Song dynasty, and held by Liaoning Provincial Museum in the caption. Extended necks, trailing legs and composed flight inform the fantasy Cloud Crane.
- [Smithsonian National Zoo, Red-crowned Crane](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/red-crowned-crane): living-crane anatomy supports the extended neck, long bill/legs and red crown. Cloud Crane's enlarged size and black outer wing tips are fantasy art direction; this is not an exact zoological reconstruction or an observed flight-performance measurement.

Lotus Sprite is the game's original lotus-valved shell and pearl-core creature;
it is not attributed to an ancient species. Four original concept images, rather
than the historical scans, were submitted to the authorized HF provider. No
museum scans or third-party modern character images ship in the build. The
historical references and actual raw-mesh inspections remain in the ignored
batch evidence directory.
